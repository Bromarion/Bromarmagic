The person behind the cards

Hi, I’m Omar.

I started performing magic on the streets of Cairo. I loved seeing strangers stop, laugh and try to figure out what had just happened.

That’s still my favorite part. I like magic you can watch from a few inches away, especially when it happens in your own hands.

Today, I perform across New Jersey, New York and Long Island. If you’re putting an event together, I’d love to hear about it.

A few things you might be wondering.

What is close up magic?

Magic performed right in front of you, often with the audience taking part. It gives small groups a chance to share the experience up close.
